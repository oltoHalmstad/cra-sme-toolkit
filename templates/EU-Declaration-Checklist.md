# EU declaration of conformity — preparation checklist (Art. 28, Annex V)
> No pre-signed sample is provided on purpose. The declaration is drawn up and signed by the manufacturer only after the applicable conformity assessment is complete.

- [ ] Product identification (name, type, batch/serial, version) matches the technical file
- [ ] Manufacturer (and authorised representative) name and address
- [ ] Statement of sole responsibility of the manufacturer
- [ ] Object of the declaration (identification allowing traceability; image where appropriate)
- [ ] Statement of conformity with relevant Union harmonisation legislation
- [ ] References to harmonised standards / common specifications / certification used (with versions)
- [ ] Notified body name, number, procedure and certificate (where applicable to the class)
- [ ] Additional information; place and date of issue; name, function and signature
- [ ] Simplified declaration (if used) points to the full declaration's internet address
- [ ] Translated into language(s) required by the Member States where made available
- [ ] Kept with technical documentation for the required retention period (TD-02)
Verify every item against the current Annex V text before use.
