# Review of CRA-SME Toolkit v1.0 against the concept note and the CRA

Reviewed 17 Oct 2026. Sources: concept note v1.0, the research summary, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Annex I list checked against a consolidated reproduction), and public tool release data. Severity: **H** fix before any pilot, **M** fix before v2, **L** improvement.

Status column: ✅ fixed in v1.1 · 🧪 covered by a test · 📋 still open (needs a human or legal decision)

| F-01 | H | Workbook › CRA Evidence Matrix P03/P04 | The Annex I Part I(2) letters are wrong. v1.0 maps access, confidentiality and integrity to (c)–(g) and puts "secure updates" under (h)–(m). The correct letters are: (c) security updates, (d) unauthorised access, (e) confidentiality, (f) integrity, (g) data minimisation, … (m) data removal. A notified body or market-surveillance reviewer would notice this at once. | The matrix is rebuilt from `data/cra_requirements_catalogue.csv`, with one row per letter from (a) to (m) | ✅ 🧪 `test_catalogue.py` |

## What already works well (keep)
- The disclaimers are honest and consistent; scores are presented as management instruments rather than legal criteria.
- The outer limits in the Art. 14 checklist (24 h, 72 h, 14 days, one month) are correct.
- The traceability-unit definition in the concept note is precise enough to turn into a schema, and v1.1 has done that.
- The Risk-Acceptance template already states that acceptance cannot waive an essential requirement.
