# E8 — Usability and adoption
**RQ5** · Instruments: SUS (Brooke 1996), with the adjective scale from Bangor et al. 2009 · `interview-guide.md` · Template `../data-templates/sus_responses.csv`

- **Task-based session (60 min):** five scripted tasks:
  1. Classify a product.
  2. Add a risk with an evidence link.
  3. Map an obligation and attach evidence.
  4. Read a gate report and decide.
  5. Draft an SRP early warning.
- **Measures:** task success, errors and time for each task; SUS after the session.
- **UTAUT constructs:** performance expectancy, effort expectancy, social influence and facilitating conditions (Venkatesh et al. 2003). Use a 5-item short scale per construct, adapted and piloted.
- **Follow-up interview (45 min)** at the end of the pilot. Code the transcript thematically (two coders, a codebook, and κ on 20% of segments).
- **Candidate predictors (RQ5):** product complexity, staff security skill, CI/CD maturity, supply-chain dependency count, and the ENISA SME maturity profile (basic, intermediate or advanced).
