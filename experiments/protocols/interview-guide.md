# Semi-structured interview guide (baseline 45 min / follow-up 45 min)
Ask for consent to record. Pseudonymise the transcript. Do not discuss unreleased vulnerabilities in detail.

## Baseline
1. **Role and context.** Your role, the product portfolio, team size, release cadence.
2. **CRA awareness.** What do you know about the CRA's timeline and obligations? Where did you learn it? (Compare with Szedlak et al. 2024 and ENISA 2026.)
3. **Classification.** How would you decide whether product X is in scope, and which class it falls in? Who decides?
4. **Risk.** Walk me through the last time you decided which security issue to fix first. What information did you use?
5. **Evidence.** If an authority asked tomorrow for proof that product X has no known exploitable vulnerability, what would you show? How long would it take?
6. **Supply chain.** How do you learn that a component you ship has a vulnerability?
7. **Reporting.** Who would decide whether an event must be reported within 24 h, and at 3 am on a Saturday?
8. **Barriers.** What would stop you from adopting a toolkit like this one? (Skills, time, CI maturity, cost, trust.)

## Follow-up
1. Which parts did you actually use, and which did you skip? Why?
2. Describe a moment when the toolkit changed a decision.
3. Where did the automated output mislead you, or where did it look complete but wasn't? (False assurance, RQ4.)
4. What did setup cost, compared with the recurring effort? (E6 triangulation.)
5. Would you keep using it after the study? What would have to change?
6. Did the tabletop exercise change your reporting procedure?
